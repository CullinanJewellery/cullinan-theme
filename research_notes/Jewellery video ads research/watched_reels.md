## What this shows
- Big brands: short (10-15 s for product reels), 3-11 shots of 1.5-3 s, one idea per reel, warm light, little or no text, logo only at the end.
- What a piece does in a reel: lifted out of the box by a hand, fastened at the neck, touched, held in the open palm, worked on by an artisan. Hands are in almost every good reel.
- Process/making-of (VCA atelier, Monica Vinader's sugar shop) gets the most engagement; sale posters (Kirkorian, Missoma) the least.
- Bulgarian competitors post still photos with emojis or phone clips: a 10-15 s clean, real-looking reel already stands out locally.
- Our first clover video (one AI shot of the piece alone, background drifting) matches none of this; the brands' reels are made of several short shots with a hand or a person.
