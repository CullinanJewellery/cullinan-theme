## The piece (confirmed by the owner, 2026-10-14)

- Thin, delicate yellow gold ring.
- **Five stones**: one at the top centre and two on each side, evenly spaced along the upper half, at about 9, 10:30,
  12, 1:30 and 3 o'clock (raw photo 29 shows all five positions). Small, round, white, each about as wide as the
  band. (The first set showed three: wrong, counted from too few photos.)
- Between neighbouring stones three to four small gold beads, slightly irregular (handmade).
- Lower half: thin, plain, polished gold band.
- **Rhodium**: the cup each stone sits in is rhodium (white) on its visible face. Seen straight from the side, only
  the rounded corners of the cup show a little white; the rest of the cup and the band are yellow gold. The two
  lowest stones' white runs a little further down into the plain band.
- Inside the band: the upper half has no separate smooth band, it is the row of beads and cups itself, so from inside
  it shows the rounded backs of the beads and a small round hole behind each stone (open back); the lower half is
  plain, smooth gold. (Corrected 2026-10-14 from raw photos 21, 28, 29, 33, owner's OK; it read "only a hole behind
  each stone, nothing else".)
- Stones face outward from the band.
