## The whole set (from the raw photos; to confirm with the owner)

- Every piece in the raw photos is **white metal** (silver or white gold, rhodium bright), one colour all over: no
  two-tone, no yellow anywhere. The photos for the site are made in yellow first (CLAUDE.md), so the yellow version
  is drawn from white raw photos: the prompt must say "all yellow gold", and whether the yellow piece has any rhodium
  must be asked (see questions).
- Each piece has **one round, brilliant-cut, clear colourless stone** in a **round bezel (обков)**: a smooth polished
  metal collar that wraps the whole girdle of the stone, no claws, no milgrain on the face, no other stones, no beads.
- The bezel is a short round **cup (tube)**; on the studs and the pendant its side wall is **open: windows cut in it,
  separated by vertical bars** (2be49e9e, a3f4d449, b44d1b37), so light reaches the stone from the side and back. On
  the ring the bezel's side looks solid in both photos, but its underside is never seen.
- Stones look clear and white in all photos; no colour cast; the type is not known (zircon? ask).

## Ring

head: f8ed7852 (straight onto the stone); for the band's shape: 9d4b7f98. **Only two photos, both from about the
same angle.**

- **Band**: plain, polished, about 2 mm wide, the same width all round; flat outer face with flat sides (a square-ish
  rectangle in section, the edges slightly softened), not domed, no texture, no pattern. Inside: plain, with a small
  stamped hallmark (lower left of the inside in 9d4b7f98 and f8ed7852), no text to copy.
- **Head**: one round bezel sitting on top of the band, its diameter about 2½ times the band's width. The bezel is a
  short upright cylinder with straight, smooth, polished outer walls; the top edge is a narrow rounded rim that folds

  the cylinder; no shoulders, no split, no decoration where they meet.
- **Stone**: one round brilliant-cut stone, clear and colourless, table facing straight out (up) from the band, sitting
  a little below the rim. Facets and sparkle visible (f8ed7852).
- Rhodium: the whole ring is white; as a yellow piece, nothing indicates any white area.
- **Not seen in any photo (missing views)**: the side profile (height of the bezel above the band, whether the bezel

  by a central bar that widens at the top and bottom (an hourglass / X shape, b44d1b37); the top edge of the wall in
  b44d1b37 shows a fine row of tool marks, not a design. Whether the back of the cup is closed or open (a cross bar

- **Stone**: one round brilliant-cut stone, clear and colourless, facing the front.
- Chain: the raw photos use a nylon thread, not a chain; nothing to copy.

## Studs (earrings)

Raw: dbc42e81 (the pair from the front, lying on white: the clearest front view), 2be49e9e (one stud seen from its
side, post down: windows in the cup), a3f4d449 (one stud from the side with its butterfly), 50b7dc99 (one stud from

- **Kind**: bezel studs, a matching pair (mirror images, same size).
- **Front**: a round bezel cup with a wide, smooth, polished collar sloping in to the stone (same look as the
  pendant's, smaller), one round brilliant-cut clear stone in the middle facing out.
- **Cup side**: a short cylinder; its wall has **windows**: in 2be49e9e and a3f4d449 a top band (the rim), a bottom
  band (the base ring) and a vertical bar in the middle, with a rectangular window on each side of it, so the side
  reads like a letter "H" turned on its side; more bars at the edges suggest four windows round the cup (ask). The
  stone's pavilion is seen through the windows.
- **Fitting**: one straight, plain post soldered at the centre of the back of the cup (on the bar), with a small
  notch near its end; a **butterfly back** (scroll back) with two curled loops and a scalloped, flower-edged plate
  (dbc42e81, 50b7dc99, a3f4d449). No hook, no lever-back.
- Size: small, a classic everyday stud (the cup about a fingertip's width across a third of it, a3f4d449).
