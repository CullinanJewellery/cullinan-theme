- Kind: pendant shaped like a bullet. It is a long cylinder, about 4.5 times as long as it is wide.

  the sides and the very end. They sit in a honeycomb pattern, each held by small round metal beads between the stones (bead
  setting). About 20 stones are visible from the front, and the metal shows only as the beads and thin walls between them.
