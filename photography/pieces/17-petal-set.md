# Set 17 — „Комплект с венчелистче“ (petal set): ring, earrings, pendant (catalogue numbers to come)

Folder: `pictures for the website/17 Комплект с венчелистче - номер предстои/{Пръстен, Обеци, Висулка}`; raw photos
in each piece's `сурови снимки` (named by their upload id). All 13 examined zoomed in on 2026-11-07 (crops 3–8×,
about 1200 px). No credits spent; nothing asked of the owner yet (see the questions at the end).

The family look: each piece is a smooth, mirror-polished gold **petal** (a teardrop or leaf shape made of two curved
sheets of gold that overlap and twist), with **one round, colourless, brilliant-cut stone** set low inside it, held
between the edges of the two petals. **No claws are visible on any piece** in any raw photo. No beads, no texture,
no engraving on the front.

## Ring

Raw: 2d5e984e 30243a44 b4f9fc39 (front, on a white card, eye level), ccdfcef3 dfe485fc (on a cup, three-quarter
from above, inside visible), df472cbc (held, seen from above). Clearest: **ccdfcef3** (head, stone, inside),
**2d5e984e** (front), dfe485fc (inside and band), df472cbc (head from above).

- **Kind:** ring with one centre stone, a bypass (crossover) design: the two ends of the band pass each other at the
  top and wrap round the stone.
- **Stone:** one round brilliant-cut colourless stone (zircon? **ask**), about two to three band widths across, set
  low in the head, table up. No other stones.
- **Head:** two wide, smooth, polished gold sheets:
  - the **upper petal** rises from the left shoulder, widens into a broad wave that arches over the top (back) edge
    of the stone and runs off to the right into the right shoulder; along it run one or two raised folds (a soft
    ridge with a groove beside it, 2d5e984e, 30243a44, close-up of 30243a44);
  - the **lower petal** rises from the right shoulder, widens into a deep, cupped, leaf-shaped shell that wraps round
    the front and left of the stone and ends in a rounded point at the lower left, tucked under the left shoulder
    (2d5e984e, ccdfcef3).
  - The stone sits in the hollow between them, its top edge just under the upper petal's rim. Nothing else holds it:
    no claws, no bezel ring, no basket, no collar (to confirm, question 2).
- **Band:** thin (about 1.5–2 mm), plain, polished, slightly flattened, with a gentle wave as it rises into each
  shoulder; it widens smoothly into the petals. Shoulders asymmetric (bypass), not split.
- **Inside:** plain polished gold with the engraved script signature „Cullinan“ low on the inside of the band and a
  small hallmark near the shoulder (dfe485fc, ccdfcef3, 30243a44). From inside under the head only the backs of the
  petals; no hole is visible behind the stone in any photo.
- **Metal:** all yellow gold. **No rhodium** anywhere (no white metal round the stone in any photo).

## Earrings

Raw: 4ed30b4a (the pair lying on a white card), 92448be6 (one hanging on a nylon thread, straight front),
577be91a ec63d966 1756660b (hanging, three-quarter from either side), 6a50273c (hanging, side, clasp open to view).
Clearest: **92448be6** (front), **577be91a** and **ec63d966** (three-quarter, stone, two-tone), **6a50273c** (clasp),
4ed30b4a (the pair).

- **Kind:** a pair of petal drops on an **English lock** (the hinged clasp common in Bulgaria: a flat gold
  U-shaped ear wire behind the earring that clicks into a rectangular catch at the top). Small, close to the ear,
  not dangling.
- **Body:** a teardrop pod, pointed at the top, round at the bottom, about 1.7× as tall as wide, made of two curved
  petals:
  - the **outer petal**, yellow gold, a smooth cupped shell that forms the round bottom and both sides; on one side
    it rises to a short pointed tip (577be91a, ec63d966);
  - the **inner petal**, a narrower tongue that rises from the bottom through the centre and twists up into the
    tallest pointed tip at the top; the tip has a small teardrop-shaped opening (92448be6, 1756660b, 577be91a).
  - The stone sits in the lower half, between the edges of the two petals, table facing forward, slightly tilted.
- **Stone:** one round brilliant-cut colourless stone per earring (zircon? **ask**), large for the piece: about
  half the pod's width.
- **Two-tone (rhodium):** the **front face of the inner petal is white** (rhodium), clearly seen: the central
  tongue from the top tip down past both sides of the stone and the strip below the stone (92448be6 front; 577be91a,
  ec63d966, 1756660b, 4ed30b4a). The outer petal, its edges, the back and the whole clasp are yellow gold. Where the
  white stops at the tip and along the edges is not certain (question 3).
- **Clasp (6a50273c, 1756660b, 577be91a):** all yellow gold. A rectangular open catch frame (a flat strip bent into a
  narrow rectangle) stands up behind the top tip; a short horizontal gold pin/bar joins the tip to it; the flat
  U-shaped ear wire runs down behind the pod and up into the catch.
- **Back of the pod:** gold; a small mark on the outer petal (577be91a, right side, middle) may be a hallmark
  (question 9).
- **Pair:** 4ed30b4a is the only photo with both; they look like mirror images but it is not certain (question 5).
