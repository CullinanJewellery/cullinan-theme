## The whole set (from the raw photos; to confirm with the owner)

- One design language: a **flat, twisting ribbon of yellow gold** (a vine or stem) that winds in S-curves, with
  **marquise-shaped (pointed oval) leaves**. The leaves with stones are **white (rhodium) on their top face**, with a
  thin raised white rim round the edge, set with small clear stones; their sides and undersides are yellow gold
  (e4caacf9, top leaf). The rest of the metal is yellow gold.
- Stones: tiny, clear, colourless, round; **how many per leaf cannot be counted** in any raw photo (each leaf is only
  about 30–90 px long at full size, lesson 44). What shows: one brighter round stone near the middle of each leaf,
  and the rest of the white plate catches light in small sparkles and dark dots (it may be an engraved "illusion"
  plate round one stone, or two to three tiny stones in a row). Ask.
- The warm pink-copper patches on the lower part of the pendant and earring (27f003d2, 41282224) look like
  reflections of a warm light, not rose gold: the same part is yellow in 92bbdb88. Confirm.
- The rows of tiny bright dots along some edges (the pendant bail's top edge in 92bbdb88; a gold leaf in ce18ffef;
  the back of the ring's band in c3f97e89) look like reflections of the ribbed lightbox and cup rim, not milgrain.
  Not to be drawn unless the owner says they are real.

## Ring

Raw: c3f97e89, 19962d9a, 1ca452bb, 0b4f5f0c (standing on white, front and three-quarter views), 2ea8d4e1, ce18ffef
(on a cup, from above and the side), e4caacf9 (on a cup, from above: the whole vine with all four white leaves).
Clearest: **c3f97e89** (front, the whole vine and leaves), **e4caacf9** (top, all four white leaves), 2ea8d4e1 and
ce18ffef (shoulders and the band), 0b4f5f0c (the back band rising into a wave).

- **Band**: slim, plain, polished yellow gold, about 1.5–2 mm wide, flat outer face with flat sides; the same width
  all round. Inside: plain, with stamped hallmarks / a maker's mark in script (2ea8d4e1, 19962d9a); nothing to copy.
- **Top (the head)**: across the top the ring is **double**: the plain band continues behind as a thin back rail,
  rising into a gentle wave (0b4f5f0c, 1ca452bb), and in front of it a **second, wavier gold ribbon (the vine)** winds
  in S-curves from one shoulder to the other, crossing in front of the back rail and touching it where they join.
  The vine is a flat polished ribbon, a little wider than the band, that twists so its face turns towards and away
  from the camera; open spaces between the vine and the back rail.
- **Leaves**: **four white leaves with stones** (e4caacf9 shows all four; c3f97e89 and 19962d9a too): marquise
  shapes, all about the same size, each growing from the vine on a short gold base:
  1. at the left end, hanging down almost vertically below the vine;
  2. upper middle, lying almost horizontally between the vine and the back rail;
  3. lower right of the middle, lying almost horizontally below the vine;
  4. at the right end, standing upright, its tip above the band.
  (Order as seen in c3f97e89; in other photos the ring is turned.)
- **Plain gold leaves**: at least **one small plain polished gold leaf without stones**, pointing down from the vine
  near leaf 1 (c3f97e89, 19962d9a, 2ea8d4e1 centre, ce18ffef centre); and probably a **second small gold leaf**
  beside leaf 4 at the right end (c3f97e89 top right, e4caacf9 top, 0b4f5f0c). Ask how many.
- **Rhodium**: only the top face of each of the four stone leaves and its thin rim: clearly white, like a white plate
  edged in white. Everything else yellow gold (vine, gold leaves, the leaves' sides and undersides, band).
- **Stones**: in each white leaf, small round clear stones; count per leaf unknown (see the set notes). The flat top
  of each leaf faces outward from the ring, so the stones face out too.
- Shots: rings with stones get no standing photo (CLAUDE.md). Main = lying flat, seen from the front a little above,
  the vine and its four white leaves along the front edge facing the camera (`../staging.md` §2, like ring C). **No
  raw photo shows the ring lying flat** (lesson 22): ask for one; c3f97e89 is the nearest view. Then the hand photo;
  white, rose and silver free.

## Pendant

Raw: **92bbdb88** (front, hanging on a nylon thread, close and sharp: the clearest), 27f003d2 (the same view, farther
and softer). Both from the same angle; no side or back view.

- **Bail**: a large plain polished bail, one flat strip folded back into a loop, wide at the top (flat rounded top
  edge) and tapering down; about a third of the pendant's whole height. Front face plain, no decoration.
- **Jump ring**: one small round ring through the lower end of the bail, seen face-on (in the pendant's plane), joining
  it to the top of the stem.
- **Stem**: one flat gold ribbon in an S: from the jump ring it rises a little to the right, curves over and down to
  the left, widens and sweeps back to the right, then turns down and ends at the bottom in a flat cut tip pointing to
  the left (a little hook). Polished; a small stamped hallmark on the face of the stem below the top curve (92bbdb88).
- **Leaf**: ONE marquise leaf, from the inside of the stem's middle curve, pointing up and to the right, at about 30°.
  Top face white rhodium with a thin white rim, small clear stone(s) in it (one round stone clearly at the left half
  in 92bbdb88; count not certain). Sides gold.
- Shots: main photo = the angle of 92bbdb88 exactly (front, hanging straight from the bail, jump ring face-on; lesson
  60: never turned or straightened); neck photo by `paste-pendant.cjs` (free).

## Earrings

Raw: 41282224 and **6b116de4 are the same file** (identical bytes); e89010d8 (same earring, turned a little, sharper).
**Only ONE earring is photographed**, hanging on a nylon thread, three-quarter from the front. No photo of the pair,
none from the side or back. Clearest: e89010d8 (the lock and the leaf), 41282224 (the satin face).

- **Fitting**: a hinged **English lock („английско закопчаване“)**: behind, a long flat gold strip in a U, with a
  rectangular window near its top (the catch); from the top of the front part a short straight wire runs back
  through the ear and clicks into that window (e89010d8, top). A hallmark is stamped on the inside of the back strip
  (41282224, lower left). No hook, no post with butterfly.
- **Front**: a flat gold ribbon that rises from the bottom of the lock, twists in an S / reversed "C" (opening towards
  the back strip), and ends at the bottom in a flat cut tip pointing to the right. **Finish: two kinds** — the edges
  and the narrow parts are polished; the wide face of the twist on the right is **satin (fine brushed, matte)**
  (41282224, e89010d8). That contrast is part of the design and must stay.
- **Leaf**: ONE marquise leaf, held inside the curve, pointing to the left towards the back strip, almost horizontal.
  White rhodium face with a thin white rim; small clear stone(s): a brighter round stone in the middle and two dark
  dots near the ends (e89010d8) — tiny stones, holes or engraving? Ask.
- Shots: main = the pair from the front, mirror images, hanging straight (`../staging.md` §3), not touching; a raw
  photo of the pair is needed first (the model cannot invent the second one reliably, and we do not know if it is a
  mirror image).
