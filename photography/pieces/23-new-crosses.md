## Разпятие (940807a4, c64a8f6f front; af92171f, 73879472 back)

- Large Orthodox cross, all yellow gold as far as seen; every arm ends in a trefoil (three round lobes); the lower arm
  is long and widens towards the bottom before its trefoil. Thin polished raised border all round.
- Front: background a fine dotted (hammered) texture; raised polished figure of the crucified Christ with a halo; small
  cross above the head; between the arms, behind the cross, dark recessed rays; letters in relief, to be copied exactly
  and nowhere else: "ЦС" (top lobe), "IC" (left), "XC" (right), "НИКА" (bottom lobe); a small skull/hill under the feet
  and a small rectangle below it.
- Back: textured ground; a banner across the arms reading "СПАСИ И СОХРАНИ"; a small ornament (a winged head?) in the
  top lobe; a candle with a flame and rays on the long arm, standing in a cup shape in the bottom lobe; arcs in the
  lobes. Bail: polished, tapered, a hallmark on its back.

## Кръст с шарка (3ca23150, one photo only)

- Small Latin cross, yellow gold; arms slightly widening, the ends cut to a shallow point; the face diamond-cut in a
  fine criss-cross (quilted) pattern with polished bevelled edges; tapered bail with a stamp. Only one photo, from the
  front: the back and the thickness are not seen.
