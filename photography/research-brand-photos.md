# How big jewellery brands photograph jewellery (research, 2026-11-10)

Asked by the owner on 2026-11-10 ("so we can make the best for our own"). Looked at live: Moon Magic, Tiffany & Co.,
Cartier, Swarovski, Pandora, Mejuri, Ole Lynggaard, Glamira (product, category and home pages, image file names and alt
text; images seen at 400–600 px). Not checked: Instagram, Van Cleef & Arpels (page redirected), Glamira worn photos.
"(interpretation)" marks a reading that could not be verified. Sources of ideas only: never copy a brand's images,
wording or signature look (no blue box, red seal, black-and-white portraits, Moon Magic's plum).

## Earrings on the ear (what they all do)
- **Angle:** a true side profile, or turned slightly away. A three-quarter view with the face is the second, more personal photo.
- **Hair:** always fully clear of the ear: tucked behind, or in a low ponytail, braid or bun. No strand crosses the lobe.
- **Face:** cropped out or cut at the eyes in the main ear photo.
- **Crop, two kinds:** ear macro (ear fills the frame: Moon Magic, Pandora), or ear, neck and collar (ear in the top
  third, neck and a collar below: Tiffany, Cartier, Swarovski).
- **Scale:** studs at real size, 3–15% of the frame width even in a macro; never enlarged.
- **Position:** the stud in the fleshy part of the lobe, facing the camera, no tilt or droop.
- **Second earring:** not seen in profile photos.
- **Clothes:** calm and neutral: white shirt collar, tailored brown or cream silk, soft knits.
- **Background:** light or warm grey studio, or a warm blurred interior.
- **Skin:** natural with visible texture at the luxury end (freckles, pores); heavy smoothing (Swarovski) looks mass-market (interpretation).

## Product photos (packshots)
- Always the first image. White or very light grey (#F2F2F2–#F7F7F7) or warm off-white, the same for every product.
- Luxury brands keep the piece small with a lot of space (pair about 30–40% of the width; Swarovski fills the frame).
- Earrings as a pair: both front-on, or one front-on and one turned to show the post. Most add a side view of one
  earring showing the post and the back (Tiffany, Cartier, Glamira, Mejuri).
- Every metal at the identical angle (Cartier, Moon Magic). Soft shadow under or just beside the piece.
- Gallery order: packshot, worn photo, close-up or side view, other metals; packaging last.
- Gold with real contrast: dark amber in the polish next to bright highlights, not flat yellow.

## Homepage category tiles
- Moon Magic: single categories (rings, necklaces, earrings, bracelets) use still lifes on one seasonal surface and
  colour with hard sun shadows; sets use worn photos. Swarovski alternates worn and still-life tiles. Tiffany and
  Cartier use campaign photos; Mejuri uses editorial still lifes.
- Moon Magic makes some product images with Higgsfield and Nano Banana (file names "hf_2026…", "Gemini 3 Nano Banana Pro").
  Glamira's product images are 3D renders and look the most CGI (perfectly identical pearls) (interpretation).

## What reads as premium and real
Empty space round a small piece; one background across a category; metal with real contrast; believable stud size on
the ear; real skin texture; hair clear of the ear; simple tailored clothes; close-ups of the post and back (trust);
the same angle for every metal.

## Recommendations for Cullinan
1. Main worn earring photo: strict profile, face cut at or below the eye, hair in a low bun or ponytail clear of the
   ear, the stud facing the camera at real size.
2. Pick one worn crop and keep it: ear, neck and collar (more premium) or ear macro (shows the piece better).
3. Natural skin with real texture; no heavy smoothing.
4. Clothes: ivory or white collar, cream silk, camel or greige knit (fits our palette).
5. Optional second worn photo: three-quarter turn, face partly visible.
6. Earring packshot on #FCFBFC: the pair small, one front-on and one turned to show the post; same angle per metal.
7. Add a side view of one earring showing the post and back (buying with confidence).
8. Pearls equal in size and lustre, but not CGI clones.
9. Home tiles: one light and colour family across all tiles so they belong together (owner asked the same, 2026-11-10).
10. Packaging photos only once packaging is confirmed (not a confirmed fact yet).
