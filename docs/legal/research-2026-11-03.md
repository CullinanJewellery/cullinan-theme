# Legal research, 2026-11-03 — what a Bulgarian online gold jeweller needs

Asked by the owner: look at Bulgarian sellers (not Glamira or moonmagic) and Bulgarian law, and list what our
policies and site are missing. **Not legal advice**: every point goes to the accountant (and a lawyer) before it is
acted on. Sources at the end.

## Bulgarian shops looked at
- **BeOrnare** (beornare.com, „СТУДИО ДИЗАЙН 66“ ООД, Велико Търново, on Shopify): the most complete. Footer: Грижа за
  бижута, Политика за връщане, Политика за доставка, Общи условия (31 sections, updated 25.10.2026), Политика за
  поверителност, **Правна информация** (Shopify's Legal notice). The legal notice shows: company, ЕИК, VAT status,
  **registration under чл. 13, ал. 1 of the Валутен закон (certificate № and issuing ministry)**, a section on
  hallmarks (state marks: wheat ear for silver, stylised rose for gold), the laws that apply, competent authorities,
  and that the EU ODR platform no longer works. Terms mention a **withdrawal function in the online interface**,
  promotions and the „предишна цена“ rule, a complaints section with what the customer should send, „случаи, които
  не представляват несъответствие“, and jewellery care.
- **Aristo Jewellery** (aristo.bg, Варна, WordPress): footer has Политика за поверителност, Условия за използване,
  Гаранционни условия, a cookie banner. The warranty covers stone setting, a faulty clasp, workmanship and a size
  correction; excludes knocks, scratches, drops, a second size correction, loss, and work done by other jewellers.
- **Kirkorian**: the page did not load its content in the browser (also on 2026-11-03 earlier).

## Law found
1. **Registration for precious metals** (Валутен закон чл. 13, ал. 1; Наредба по ПМС № 181/2004): a trader who
   makes or sells precious-metal items by occupation must be entered in the public register, now kept by the
   Ministry of Economy (Министерство на икономиката и индустрията), before starting; a copy of the certificate is
   displayed in every shop and workshop (Наредба чл. 2, ал. 12).
2. **Hallmarking** (Наредба чл. 41, 52, 54–58): gold and silver items for sale are tested and marked by НАП's
   precious-metals laboratories (state mark: rose for gold, wheat ear for silver) and carry the maker's mark;
   **unmarked items may not be sold or handed to customers** (чл. 58). Shops display an information board with the
   state marks (чл. 41, ал. 4, Приложение 3а).
3. **Certificate** (Наредба чл. 17, ал. 2): gold and silver items are sold with a certificate of the alloy's
   fineness. Stones: a certificate of identity and quality from a registered Bulgarian enterprise or an approved
   foreign institute (чл. 18, ал. 1). Weight and fineness are written on the invoice or receipt (чл. 59, ал. 1).
4. **Sales documents and НАП** (Наредба Н-18, changes of 2020): payment by postal money transfer (courier cash on
   delivery) needs no fiscal device, but the customer must get a document with the seller's name and address, the
   goods, tax group, quantity, value, total and payment method. Card payments without a fiscal receipt need the
   e-shop registered with НАП (Приложение 33) and a monthly audit file (Приложение 38). For the accountant.
5. **EU withdrawal button** (Directive (EU) 2023/2673, applies from 19 June 2026): an online shop must offer a
   clearly labelled function to withdraw from the contract online („Откажете се от договора тук“ or similar),
   available for the whole 14 days, and confirm receipt to the customer. It does not apply to contracts with no
   right of withdrawal (made to order, engraved). Bulgarian transposition text not found.
6. **Product safety** (Regulation (EU) 2023/988, GPSR, art. 19, since 13 December 2024): every online product offer
   shows the manufacturer's name or trade mark, postal and electronic address, product identification (picture,
   type) and any warnings.
7. **Chemicals in jewellery** (REACH, Annex XVII): nickel release limits for items in long contact with skin (entry
   27; stricter for piercing posts), cadmium (entry 23) and lead (entry 63) in jewellery. White gold alloys may
   contain nickel: ask the atelier which alloy is used.
8. **Cash limit** (Закон за ограничаване на плащанията в брой): payments of 10,000 лв. or more only by bank transfer;
   the euro figure after 1 January 2026 was not confirmed. Expensive pieces on cash on delivery may reach it.
9. **ЗЗП**: complaints are entered in a register and the customer gets a document with its number; price reductions
   show the lowest price of the previous 30 days; whether reviews are checked must be stated (already in the terms
   draft).
