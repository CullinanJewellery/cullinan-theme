`custom.dimensions`, stone fields; see `docs/tasks.md` §B) and working out prices in the calculator.

(`Pictures/PicturesForWebFaceInstagram/pictures for the website/<NN piece>/`) and the piece sheets in
`photography/pieces/`. All weights are **14K gold**; 18K weights not given yet. **—** = not given yet (ask the owner).
